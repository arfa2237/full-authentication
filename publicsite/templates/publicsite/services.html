How It Works

Our Core Services — In Detail

Each service is designed to make your vaccination journey smooth, trackable, and stress-free.

Schedule Appointments

Book your vaccination appointment online in just 3 simple steps — anytime, anywhere

How Scheduling Works
  • 1
    Choose Your Vaccine Select from 6 available vaccine types — Hepatitis B, MMR, Tetanus, Pneumococcal, Child Vaccination, or COVID-19.
  • 2
    Select a Center & Time Choose your nearest vaccination center and pick a convenient date and time slot.
  • 3
    Get Confirmation Healthcare staff reviews your request and confirms. You receive an automatic email notification once confirmed.
  • 4
    Visit & Get Vaccinated Arrive at your scheduled time. Staff marks your appointment complete and your digital record updates instantly.
Why It Matters for Pakistan

Pakistan achieved its highest-ever DTP3 coverage at 87% in 2024 — a major milestone for the country's immunization programme.

  However, Punjab has 92% childhood immunization coverage vs 82.5% nationally. Digital scheduling helps close this gap.

TIG's online appointment system removes travel barriers and long wait times — making vaccination accessible for every citizen of Gujranwala.

Track Your Records

Monitor your complete vaccination history and immunization progress in real-time

What You Can Track
  • ✓
    Appointment Status See all your appointments — Pending, Scheduled, Completed, Missed, or Cancelled — in one place.
  • ✓
    Dose Progress Per Vaccine Visual progress bars show how many doses you have completed out of the total required for each vaccine.
  • ✓
    Immunization Percentage A circular progress indicator shows your overall immunization completion out of 13 recommended doses.
  • ✓
    Statistical Charts Interactive donut and bar charts powered by Chart.js give you a visual summary of your vaccination history.
Digital Vaccine Card

Every registered patient receives a unique PIN (TIG-XXXX-GRW) and a digital vaccination card.

  • View all completed vaccinations in a clean table
  • Download your vaccine card as a PDF certificate
  • Share or print it for medical records
  • Always accessible — no clinic visit required

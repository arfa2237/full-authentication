{% trans "Vaccines are biological preparations that provide active acquired immunity to specific infectious diseases. They contain agents that resemble disease-causing microorganisms and are often made from weakened or killed forms of the microbe." %}

{% trans "How they work:" %} {% trans "When administered, vaccines stimulate the body's immune system to recognize the agent as a threat, destroy it, and remember it. This creates immunological memory that allows the immune system to more easily recognize and destroy any of these microorganisms that it later encounters." %}

{% trans "WHO Reference:" %} {% trans "Vaccines train your immune system to create antibodies, just as it does when exposed to a disease. However, because vaccines contain only killed or weakened forms of germs, they do not cause the disease or put you at risk of its complications." %}

{% trans "Vaccination is one of the most effective ways to prevent diseases and save lives. Here's why it's crucial:" %}

  • {% trans "Disease Prevention:" %} {% trans "Prevents 3.5-5 million deaths annually from diseases like diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis, influenza and measles" %}
  • {% trans "Herd Immunity:" %} {% trans "Protects vulnerable populations who cannot be vaccinated (newborns, elderly, immunocompromised)" %}
  • {% trans "Disease Eradication:" %} {% trans "Has successfully eradicated smallpox and nearly eliminated polio worldwide" %}
  • {% trans "Cost-Effective:" %} {% trans "Saves healthcare costs by preventing expensive treatments and hospitalizations" %}
  • {% trans "Future Protection:" %} {% trans "Provides long-term immunity and protects future generations" %}

  • {% trans "Phase 1 Trials:" %} {% trans "Small group safety testing (20-100 people)" %}
  • {% trans "Phase 2 Trials:" %} {% trans "Expanded safety and dosage studies (several hundred people)" %}

{% trans "Most vaccine side effects are mild and temporary, indicating that the body is building protection:" %}

{% trans "Common Mild Reactions:" %} {% trans "These usually last 1-2 days and include soreness at injection site, mild fever, fatigue, fussiness (in children), and loss of appetite." %}

  • {% trans "Infants:" %} {% trans "Sleepiness, temporary appetite loss, mild fever" %}
  • {% trans "Children:" %} {% trans "Sore arm, low-grade fever, mild rash" %}
